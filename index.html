Getting an accurate reading

Light it wellCamera measuring struggles in dim rooms. Switch the big light on.
Give it texturePlain white walls confuse the camera. Aim at edges, handles and corners.
Move slowlySweep the phone gently before tapping so it can map the room.
Case off for levellingA bulky case or camera bump can tilt the phone by a degree or more.
Measure twicePhone readings are good to a centimetre or two. Use a tape before you cut anything.
