Handy to know

1 inch is exactly 25.4 mm, and 1 foot is 304.8 mm.

UK timber and sheet materials are sold in metric, but many sizes are old imperial ones rounded: a 2.4 m length was 8 feet, and a 1220 × 2440 mm sheet was 4 × 8 feet.

Plasterboard studs are usually 400 or 600 mm apart, close to the old 16 and 24 inches.
