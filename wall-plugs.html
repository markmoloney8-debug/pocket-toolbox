Wall plug picker

Pick your wall and what you're hanging to see which fixing, drill bit and screw to use. Brand-neutral: any good make of the same type will do.

Your wall
Not sure what your wall is?

Knock on it. A dull, hard thud means solid brick or block. A hollow sound means plasterboard. Hollow in some places and solid in others usually means plasterboard stuck onto brick (dot and dab).

Drill a small test hole and watch the dust. Red or orange dust means brick. Grey dust that drills very easily means aerated block. White dust that suddenly stops means plasterboard over a gap. White dust, then a gap, then red or grey dust means dot and dab.

Think about the age of the house. Homes built before about 1930 often have lath and plaster: thin wooden strips under lumpy plaster. The drill catches on wood and the surface can feel slightly uneven.

What you're hanging
Check for cables and pipes first In UK homes, cables usually run straight up, down or sideways from sockets and switches, and within 150 mm of the ceiling and of wall corners. Don't drill in those zones, and run a cable and pipe detector over the spot before you start.
