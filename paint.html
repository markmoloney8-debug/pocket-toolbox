Tips

Check the coverage on the tin. Most emulsions cover 10 to 13 m² per litre, and cheaper paints often cover less.

Changing from a dark colour to a light one, or the other way, may need a third coat.

New plaster soaks up paint. Thin the first coat with about 10–20% water, or as the tin says, then do your normal coats.

Buy everything for one room at once so the colour matches. Batches can vary slightly.
