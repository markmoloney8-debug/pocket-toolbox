Try it here

How to use it

  1. Tap Start level. On an iPhone, choose Allow when it asks about motion.
  2. Lay the phone flat on the surface, or hold its long edge against a shelf or picture frame.
  3. Adjust until the bubble sits in the middle ring and turns green. That's within half a degree.

Take a bulky case off first. A case or camera bump can tilt the phone by a degree or more. If your phone reads slightly off on a surface you know is level, tap Set as zero there.
