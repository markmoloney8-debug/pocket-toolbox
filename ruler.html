Measure small things like screws, buttons and hinges. Set it to actual size once with a bank card.

On a phone, use the full-screen ruler. It runs along the longest edge of your screen, so it's just as long whether you hold the phone upright or sideways.

Set up with a bank card

Every screen is a different size, so do this once on each device. Hold a bank or loyalty card upright against the screen, with its left edge lined up with the left edge of your screen. Adjust until the box is exactly as wide as the card's short edge.
